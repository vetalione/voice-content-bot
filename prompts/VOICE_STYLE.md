# VOICE STYLE

Relevant sections are injected per stage. Editors share tone and exclusions;
Threads/Reels receive only their own examples. The miner receives exclusions only.
It is the single most effective place to make output sound like you.

Fill it in gradually. Real examples beat abstract adjectives — two or three of
your actual posts pasted below will change the output more than a page of
adjectives. Anything you leave as a placeholder is simply ignored, so it is safe
to edit a little at a time.

No restart is needed after editing in most setups (see `PROMPT_CACHE` note in the
README); on Render, redeploy or restart the service to be certain.
